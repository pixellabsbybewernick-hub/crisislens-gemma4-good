## Project title

CrisisLens — Local AI Field Reports with Gemma 4

## Short description

CrisisLens converts a crisis photo and messy field note into a structured dispatch report, deterministic routing recommendation, and SMS/radio-ready summary using Gemma 4 in a local-first workflow.

## Impact area

Primary: Global Resilience  
Secondary: Safety & Trust, Digital Equity  
Special technology angle: Ollama / Local Ops

## Demo sentence

A responder uploads a flood or landslide photo, adds a short field note, and CrisisLens produces an urgency level, missing-information checklist, safety notes, routing output, and SMS-ready field message for human verification.

## Technical sentence

Gemma 4 performs multimodal report extraction into strict JSON; a deterministic Python routing tool maps the report to teams and review urgency; Streamlit provides the coordinator UI; Ollama enables local-first execution.

## Safety sentence

CrisisLens does not identify people, does not make medical diagnoses, preserves uncertainty, asks for missing information, and requires human review before action.
